# Locks

Who is currently revising which part of this repository. Follow
[Playbook coordination](https://github.com/gantemur/playbook/blob/main/coordination.md).
Claims and releases are recorded on `main`; an empty table means nothing is locked.
A lock coordinates writes but does not authorize publication or merging.
